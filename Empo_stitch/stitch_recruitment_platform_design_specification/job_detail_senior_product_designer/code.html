Senior Product Designer

business TechNova location_on San Francisco, CA (Remote Friendly) payments $140k – $180k + Equity

info About the Role

At TechNova, we are building the next generation of enterprise SaaS tools that empower global teams to collaborate seamlessly. As a Senior Product Designer, you will be a core member of our product organization, shaping the visual identity and user experience of our flagship platform. You'll bridge the gap between complex technical requirements and intuitive, delightful interfaces. This role is perfect for a designer who thrives on solving ambiguous problems and is passionate about pushing the boundaries of what enterprise software can feel like.
