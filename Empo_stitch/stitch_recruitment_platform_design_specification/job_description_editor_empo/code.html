About the Role

We are seeking a visionary Senior Product Designer to join our core product team. In this role, you will be responsible for defining the user experience across our web and mobile platforms, working closely with engineering and product management to ship world-class features.

Key Responsibilities

  • Lead the end-to-end design process, from discovery to high-fidelity prototyping.
  • Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define product requirements.
  • Maintain and evolve our design system, ensuring visual consistency across all touchpoints.
  • Conduct user research and usability testing to validate design decisions.

Qualifications

The ideal candidate has 5+ years of experience designing complex SaaS products. You should have a deep understanding of UX principles, a strong eye for visual design, and experience working in an agile environment.
