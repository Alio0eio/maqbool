# Brag Plan: Maqbool (marketing-site hero video)

## What is this app?
Maqbool is an AI-native recruitment platform: candidates answer video interviews on their own time, and recruiters get a ranked shortlist with the AI's reasoning shown.

## The angle
A quiet, premium product film where the **logo is the protagonist**. The Maqbool mark's ring is drawn like a camera record ring, then becomes the viewfinder that frames the interview. The check strokes in when the AI "approves". The same mark closes the film as the full lockup. "Hiring, heard clearly." is the one claim; every scene is the working product proving it.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Navy void. A thin ring draws itself (stroke-dash) around a point, 0.0-1.0s. The steel check strokes in inside it (1.0-1.6s). Wordmark lockup resolves: "maqb" and "ol" slide in either side of the mark (1.6-2.4s). Tagline "Hiring, heard clearly." fades up below and holds (2.4-3.5s).

## Key moments (the middle)
- Candidate recording an async answer: amber viewfinder corner brackets snap on, REC dot, mono timer counting 00:08 → 00:21, waveform bars reacting, "Question 2 of 5" label.
- AI-ranked shortlist: four candidate rows arrive one by one, each with a score ring that counts up; top row expands to show the AI reasoning line.
- Side-by-side comparison matrix: three candidates, bars filling for skill match / experience match / AI overall; leader gets a check stroke (the logo's check motif).
- Completion monitor: per-role completion bars fill (illustrative data, no headline % claim).

## Outro / punchline
Everything dissolves back into the ring. Lockup returns large, "Hiring, heard clearly." beneath, then a pill button "Try the live demo →" with a cursor click. End on the lockup.

## User flow worth showing
Entry → key action → result: (1) a candidate records an answer on their own time, (2) the recruiter opens the AI-ranked shortlist and sees reasoning, (3) compares top candidates and invites. Scenes 2-4 are this flow.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film, logo-led motion graphic
- Interpretation: fewer, longer holds, soft crossfades and slides, no jokes, restraint on SFX; motion is precise and springy, never loud.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 25s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #00236f navy (gradient to hsl(215 95% 30%)); dark stage hsl(222 45% 8%) for UI panels
- Accent: #EB9C2C amber (record/viewfinder only); steel #0058be and light steel #7DB6F2 (logo check on dark)
- Text: #FFFFFF on navy; #111c2d on light cards
- Display font: Inter (600/700, tracking -0.035em)
- Body font: Inter; wordmark Barlow 500; JetBrains Mono for timers/scores
- Strongest visual element: the circle-and-check mark; the viewfinder corner brackets; the waveform

Fictional stand-ins: candidate names and scores come from the app's own mock data (Alex Rivera 94, Casey Jordan 88, Jamie Chen 85; role Senior Product Designer at "Stratos Financial", itself fictional). No real people, emails or customer data appear.

## Share copy (draft)
Hiring, heard clearly. Meet Maqbool: async video interviews and AI shortlists that show their reasoning.

## Audio direction
- Role: sparse professional accents over a warm bed
- Music: bundled "Happy Beats / Business Moves vol. 9" (~115 BPM)
- Music treatment: starts at 0:00 low, swells gently into scene 2, ducks slightly under the CTA, fade-out over final 1.5s
- Music cue guidance: preset read. Strong cues: 3.70s (scene 2 entry), 6.34s, 10.54s (scene 3 list reveal), 12.65s; beat grid ~0.53s. Scene 3 row arrivals snap to every other beat (~1.06s) so text stays readable. 23.17s strong cue lands the CTA/lockup.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; music RMS/bass makes the logo glow and the viewfinder frame breathe. No equalizer visuals (the on-screen waveform is product UI, not a visualizer).
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched: soft tick on ring close, UI tap on check, card "pop" per row, click on CTA.
- Audio-coupled moments: ring/check draw, shortlist rows one by one, cursor click on CTA.
- Restraint rule: no impacts, risers, or casino sounds; nothing louder than the music bed.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Mark — 3.5s (0.0-3.5)
Ring draws, check strokes, "maqb" + mark + "ol" lockup resolves, tagline "Hiring, heard clearly." holds ≥1.3s.
Sequential/interaction: yes — ring → check → wordmark halves → tagline, one after another
Audio intent: calm, confident arrival
Audio-coupled idea: soft tick when the ring closes, light tap when the check lands
Music: warm bed enters
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2 (ring expands into viewfinder frame)

### Scene 2 — Async interview — 5.5s (3.5-9.0)
Viewfinder brackets (amber) snap around a candidate panel for Alex Rivera, "Senior Product Designer · Stratos Financial". REC dot, timer 00:08→00:21 in mono, waveform bars, "Question 2 of 5". Caption: "Candidates answer on their own time." (hold ≥1.3s)
Sequential/interaction: yes — question chips tick 1→2 and the timer counts
Audio intent: focused, human
Audio-coupled idea: waveform lifts on the bass; soft record tick
Music: bed swells
Transition mood: slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — AI-ranked shortlist — 6s (9.0-15.0)

Sequential/interaction: yes — 4 rows one by one, then the top row expands and reasoning types out
Audio intent: precise, satisfying
Audio-coupled idea: card pop per row on the beat grid
Music: bed steady
Transition mood: clean → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Compare — 4s (15.0-19.0)
Three-column matrix: skill match / experience match / AI overall as filling bars. Leader column gets a check stroke. Caption: "Compare side by side." (hold ≥1.0s)
Sequential/interaction: yes — bars fill column by column
Audio intent: clarity
Audio-coupled idea: tick as each bar lands
Music: bed steady
Transition mood: slide → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Completion monitor — 3s (19.0-22.0)
Per-role completion bars fill: Senior Product Designer, Frontend Engineer, UX Researcher (illustrative). Caption: "See who's finished, at a glance." (hold ≥1.0s)

Audio-coupled idea: none (keep the bed clean)

### Scene 6 — Lockup + CTA — 3s (22.0-25.0)

Audio intent: resolved, warm
Audio-coupled idea: click SFX on the pill
Music: ducks slightly, fades out over the last 1.5s
Transition mood: end
