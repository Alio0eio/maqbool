About the Role

Lumina Tech is looking for a Senior Product Designer to join our core product team. You will be responsible for defining the future of how professionals collaborate across decentralized networks. This isn't just about making things look pretty—it's about solving complex interaction challenges at the intersection of productivity and AI.

As a senior member of the team, you'll own major initiatives from discovery through to high-fidelity implementation. You'll partner closely with engineering and product management to ensure our solutions are not only beautiful but feasible and impactful.

Key Responsibilities

  • check_circle Lead the design of complex features from conceptual ideation to polished interactive prototypes.
  • check_circle Conduct user research and usability testing to validate design decisions and iterate based on real feedback.
  • check_circle Contribute to and evolve our design system, ensuring consistency across all touchpoints.
  • check_circle Mentor junior and mid-level designers, fostering a culture of excellence and continuous learning.
  • check_circle Collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders to align design strategy with business objectives.

Qualifications

  • verified 5+ years of experience in product design, ideally in high-growth SaaS environments.
  • verified A strong portfolio showcasing complex systems thinking and exceptional visual craft.
  • verified Proficiency with modern design tools like Figma, Framer, and Adobe Creative Cloud.
  • verified Strong understanding of frontend technologies (HTML/CSS/JS) to facilitate handoffs.
  • verified Excellent communication skills and the ability to articulate design rationale to non-designers.
